How it works

A structured process from first conversation to completed deal, built on understanding your objectives and aligning the right partners, not simply matching you with the first available capital.

You reach out

Tell us about your business, project or mandate. Every conversation is treated with complete confidentiality.

Common Enquiries

A few things businesses and funding partners often ask. If your question isn't here, contact our team.

Are you a lender or a fund?

No. Zentria originates and structures transactions, then connects you with the right funding partners from our network. Because we're independent, we're never tied to a single source of capital.

Who do you work with?

On one side, established businesses and project sponsors seeking strategic capital. On the other, private investors, family offices and institutional providers looking for well-structured opportunities.

How do you protect confidentiality?

Discretion is the default. Every conversation is treated in strict confidence, and your information is only ever shared with your consent.

How do you charge?

We agree clear, transparent terms before any work begins, so you always understand how we're engaged before committing to anything.
