A real estate project brings together a location, a delivery team and a plan for how a place will be used. At TGM Invest, we consider those elements together when exploring a potential partnership.

## Start with the purpose of the place

Who will live or work there? What does the surrounding area need? We look for a clear explanation of demand and a project that responds to it.

## Understand the path to delivery

Planning, construction and procurement each bring dependencies. We want to understand the current position, the remaining decisions and the experience of the people responsible for delivering the project.

## Discuss what could change

Costs and timelines are assumptions, not certainties. A useful discussion considers delays, changes in demand and the options available if the original plan needs to adapt.

## Agree how to work together

A partnership needs clarity about responsibilities, decisions and reporting. These conversations help establish how the developer and investment partner will respond as the project progresses.

For us, a compelling proposal combines a thoughtful use for a site with a realistic delivery plan and people prepared to work through the details.
