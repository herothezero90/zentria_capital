Most funding conversations start with an amount. The better ones start with a question: what are you prepared to exchange for the capital?

## Debt: keep control, commit cash flow

Debt keeps your ownership intact, but it puts a fixed claim on your future cash flow. It suits businesses with predictable revenues and projects with clear exit values.

## Equity: share upside, share the table

Equity investors take risk alongside you, which makes the capital more patient — but they expect a voice in how the business is run and a share of what it becomes.

## Joint venture: bring a partner, not just a cheque

For large projects, a joint venture can bring capital and capability at the same time. The right JV partner adds expertise, relationships and credibility that pure funding cannot.

There is no universally correct answer — only the structure that best matches your objectives, your risk tolerance and your timeline.
