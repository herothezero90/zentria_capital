An investment conversation starts with the people behind the plan. At TGM Invest, we want to understand what you have built, where you want to take it and why this is the right time to bring in a partner.

## Explain the business simply

Describe your customers, what they value and how your business earns its revenue. A straightforward explanation gives the conversation a useful foundation before anyone opens a spreadsheet.

## Connect the investment to a plan

Be specific about what the capital would support: expanding a team, acquiring a complementary business or developing a property. Explain the milestones you want to reach and the assumptions behind them.

## Make room for the difficult questions

Every plan has uncertainties. Customer concentration, delivery capacity and project timing are all worth discussing early. Being open about the challenges helps both sides understand what needs more work.

## Describe the partnership you want

Capital is only one part of the relationship. Think about the involvement you would welcome, the decisions you want to retain and how you would define progress together.

The first conversation does not need to resolve everything. It should give both sides enough clarity to decide whether to take the next step.
